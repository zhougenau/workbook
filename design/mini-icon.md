# Retained Form

Knowledge becomes visible through an open, generous silhouette. Two facing planes meet at a single spine, creating a small architecture of attention. The composition is meticulously crafted around one recognizable gesture rather than an accumulation of symbols.

A deep botanical green carries the quiet weight of repetition. Near-white planes establish immediate contrast, while a restrained vermilion accent marks a moment of recognition. Color has a job: each tone names a different level of the visual hierarchy.

The alphabet is treated as geometry, not a caption. One sturdy letter stands for a larger language; one decisive check stands for recall. Painstaking attention to stroke weight and optical spacing keeps these gestures distinct when the canvas is reduced.

Empty space protects the central form. The outer field remains uninterrupted so that circular crops and small displays preserve the same balanced impression. Master-level execution here means clarity: crisp edges, deliberate asymmetry, and nothing that must be explained before it can be seen.
